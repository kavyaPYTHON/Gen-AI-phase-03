MACROOPS AI – DELIVERY DELAY STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

Purpose:
This SOP provides a structured procedure for handling delivery delays and reducing SLA breaches.

1. IDENTIFY THE ISSUE
- Identify orders that have exceeded or are at risk of exceeding the promised delivery time.
- Check the current delivery delay in minutes.
- Check the SLA status of the order.

2. CHECK DELIVERY ASSIGNMENT
- Verify whether a rider has been assigned.
- Check the rider assignment delay.
- Check whether the assigned rider is available to complete the delivery.

3. CHECK DELIVERY CONDITIONS
- Review the delivery distance.
- Check whether the delay is related to rider assignment, operational workload, or delivery conditions.
- Identify whether similar delays are affecting multiple orders.

4. PRIORITIZE THE ORDER
- High-risk or SLA-breaching orders should receive immediate attention.
- Prioritize orders that are close to or beyond their promised delivery time.

5. TAKE CORRECTIVE ACTION
- Contact or reassign the rider when required.
- Prioritize delayed orders for fulfillment and delivery.
- Escalate repeated or severe delivery delays to the operations manager.

6. RECORD THE RESOLUTION
- Record the reason for the delay.
- Record the corrective action taken.
- Monitor the order until the issue is resolved.

7. PREVENTIVE ACTION
- Monitor assignment delays regularly.
- Identify recurring delivery bottlenecks.
- Review workforce and rider availability.
- Use SLA risk and exception information to identify potential problems early.

IMPORTANT:
This SOP is a project-level operational knowledge document for the MacroOps AI prototype. Operational actions should be reviewed and approved by the responsible manager.
